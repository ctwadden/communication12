How to use this

A mood board collects the colours, type, and images that capture the feeling of your project — before you start designing.

1
Read the brief
It's pinned at the top of the canvas. Write your creative direction there.
2
Add your pieces
Use the left tools: Image (upload / paste / search), Color (pick or extract from a photo), Type (font pairings), Note (sticky notes).
3
Arrange it
Drag pieces around. Drag the orange dot at the corner to resize.
4
Explain your choices
Click the green ● on any piece to write why you chose it. This is the part that's graded — turn the dot orange.
5
SAVE so you don't lose work
Click Save Project → it downloads a file. Move that file into your Google Drive. Next class, click Open Project and choose it to keep working.
This is manual — save before you close the tab, every class.
6
Hand it in
When finished, click Export PDF (or PNG) and submit that.

Outcome Checklist ▴

  • Board shows a clear, intentional creative direction (not a random collection).
  • A deliberate colour palette is present and justified.
  • Typography choices suit the audience and message.
  • Imagery is appropriate, original or properly sourced.
  • Each key element has a written rationale (the ● buttons).
  • Composition demonstrates design principles (balance, contrast, hierarchy).

Welcome to Mood Board Studio

A mood board collects the colours, fonts, and images that capture the feeling of your project. Here's the quick version:

  1. Add images, colours, type, and notes from the tools on the left.
  2. Arrange them by dragging; resize from the corner dot.
  3. Click the ● on a piece to explain why you chose it — that's the graded part.
  4. Save Project → put the file in your Google Drive → Open Project next class. Save before you close the tab!
  5. Export PDF to hand it in.
